Youth Lens begins in Dortmund

The partnership met in Dortmund for its first Transnational Project Meeting.

Activity 1Dortmund · GermanyCHANCENGLEICH · YENGEC · Montemar

Youth Lens officially began in Dortmund, where CHANCENGLEICH, YENGEC and Montemar came together for the project's first Transnational Project Meeting.

From planning to action

Across two working days, the partners moved from planning to action: aligning the project methodology, shaping the Youth Lens Toolkit and agreeing how youth participation, media literacy, inclusion, safeguarding and consent will guide the work ahead.

The meeting clarified partner roles, quality and evaluation arrangements, and the practical roadmap for local capacity-building, piloting and youth film-making across Germany, Türkiye and Spain.

Three countries. One shared methodology. Many young voices still to come.

What we worked on

  • A shared Youth Lens methodology for participatory documentary storytelling
  • The structure and first development steps for the Youth Lens Toolkit
  • Media literacy, youth voice and civic/community storytelling
  • Inclusion and meaningful participation of young people with fewer opportunities
  • Safeguarding, informed consent, GDPR and responsible publishing
  • The roadmap for local workshops, piloting and youth mini-documentaries

What comes next?

The next stage is Activity 2. Partners will develop the practical training cycle and first pilot-ready version of the Toolkit, prepare youth workers to use the approach, and then begin local workshops with young people.

The shared goal is clear: strengthen skills and confidence while creating space for young people—especially those with fewer opportunities—to speak about the issues that matter to them through film and digital storytelling.
