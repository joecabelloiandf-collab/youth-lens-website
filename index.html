About the project

Turning youth voice into meaningful digital stories

Youth Lens is a 16-month Erasmus+ Youth project using participatory documentary storytelling as a practical youth-work method. It helps young people aged 16–25 build confidence, media literacy, communication skills and civic participation.

The project has a strong inclusion focus, especially supporting young people with fewer opportunities through local delivery, accessible smartphone-based tools and flexible creative roles.

Project roadmap

Four activities. One learning journey.

A1
Completed

Kick-off & Project Foundations

Dortmund, Germany. Shared methodology, quality standards, safeguarding, consent, evaluation and implementation plan.

A2
In progress

Local Capacity-Building Workshops

October 2026 – May 2027. Building the Youth Lens methodology and Toolkit, preparing youth workers and testing the training cycle locally.

A3
Next

Local Piloting & Youth Film-Making

June 2027 – January 2028. Young people create 2–3 mini-documentaries per country through structured local piloting.

A4
Final stage

Validation, Film Showcases & Closure

January 2028. Final Toolkit, impact report, local film showcases and sustainability commitments.
